# Editorial wave 5 — phonetic coverage + selective materialization

This loop deliberately mixes three sources instead of following only the highest-yield queue positions: homophone differentials, strong one/two-syllable visual concepts, and a small quota from phonetic zones that were sparse among the 98 curated hypotheses.

## Descriptive phonetic gaps before the wave

A one-off descriptive count over the current representation landscape showed notably sparse initial buckets: `/o…/` 0 curated / 62 mapped, `/g…/` 1 / 184, `/l…/` 2 / 202, `/ʁ…/` 4 / 350, and `/n…/` 2 / 143. Only 36 of the 98 curated sounds were two-syllable windows. These observations are sampling guidance, not a new score or validation framework.

Wave 5 examines 36 sounds: 18 from the phonetic-gap quota and 18 from yield/homophone-differential review. It compares 65 exact lexical candidates, promotes 21 editorial hypotheses, defers 15, and promotes 3 two-syllable windows. Examples of useful differentials include `lire → lyre`, `accro → accroc`, and `marchant → marchand`. Strong single-word coverage additions include `grain`, `lance`, `chou`, `nuit`, `ciel`, `yeux`, `sable`, `tasse`, and `tronc`.

The gap quota does not force success. `autan`, `guerre` and `ré` are explicitly deferred because the representation route is not clean enough. In particular the `/o…/` bucket remains a genuine product gap rather than being padded with a weak image.

## Materialization wave 2

Eight already-curated prototypes were confronted with actual graphic production after checking the repository tree, registered assets and research prototypes. Four small research-only SVGs were created: `pelle`, `radis`, `carré`, and the chess sense of `fou`. `loup`, `leurre`, and `pêcher` were returned for rework after graphic inspection; `pâté` was abandoned for this route.

The four new SVGs stay in `assets/research`. They are concrete stimuli to inspect, not registered production assets and not evidence of naming. Every inspected item keeps `spontaneousNamingRisk = unknown`, `humanNamingEvidence = none`, and `clinicalEvidence = none`.

The audited materialized floor therefore moves from 8 to 12 curated sounds with a concrete graphic stimulus, and from 11 to 15 when previously-qualified visible conventions are included. These are conservative floors, not exhaustive claims over every historical asset.
